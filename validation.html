Validation Studies

Four preprints on Zenodo benchmark AnalyVa's engines against R 4.4.0, SmartPLS 4, and the 31 NIST StRD certified datasets. Every dataset, script, and output file used in these studies is public.

by Abdelouahd Bouzar & Khaoula El Idrissi · July 2026

Study 1 · General statistics engine

AnalyVa vs R 4.4.0 — nine benchmark studies plus NIST StRD

Descriptive statistics, correlations, regression, ANOVA, and factor analysis benchmarked on iris, mtcars, simulated survey data (nine studies total), plus the 31 certified reference datasets published by the U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST StRD). Results reproduce R's output to numerical precision.

Read on Zenodo Reproduce (OSF) DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21536912
Study 2 · PLS-SEM engine

AnalyVa vs SmartPLS 4 — moderation on the corporate reputation dataset (n = 344)

Three moderation-model specifications benchmarked on the canonical corporate reputation dataset from the Hair et al. PLS-SEM textbook (n = 344): a simple four-construct reflective model, an extended moderation model with a multi-item moderator, and a multiple-interaction model. Absolute differences in path coefficients stay below .010 across all three models; outer loadings match to within .006.

Read on Zenodo Reproduce (Mendeley Data) DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21536948
Study 3 · SEM engines

CB-SEM (Wheaton alienation, N = 932) and PLS-SEM (burnout, N = 592)

Two benchmarks in one paper. CB-SEM: the Wheaton alienation model (AMOS Example 6, N = 932), a classical identification-and-fit test. PLS-SEM: a ten-construct burnout model (N = 592). AnalyVa reproduces SmartPLS 4 numerics and model-fit indices in both cases.

Read on Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21537010
Study 4 · Advanced techniques

CFA, configural MGA, latent growth, and endogeneity correction

Five studies in one paper. CFA and configural MGA on the Holzinger–Swineford dataset. Latent growth curve modelling. Gaussian copula endogeneity correction in both OLS and PLS-SEM. Each result cross-checked against SmartPLS 4.

Read on Zenodo Reproduce (OSF) DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21537354
