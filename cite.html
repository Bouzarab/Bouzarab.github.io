Citation

How to Cite AnalyVa

Copy-paste citations for AnalyVa and the four validation studies. If your reference manager accepts BibTeX or RIS, use those directly.

Citing AnalyVa (the software)

Cite the software any time you use it to produce results in a paper, thesis, dissertation, poster, or report. If you also relied on benchmark evidence from one of the validation studies, cite that study in addition (see below).

APA (7th edition)

APA 7 
Bouzar, A. (2026). AnalyVa (Version 4.2.2) [Computer software]. https://analyva.com

MLA (9th edition)

MLA 9 
Bouzar, Abdelouahd. AnalyVa. Version 4.2.2, 2026, analyva.com.

Chicago (author-date)

Chicago 
Bouzar, Abdelouahd. 2026. AnalyVa (version 4.2.2). https://analyva.com.

BibTeX

BibTeX 
@software{bouzar_analyva_2026,
  author  = {Bouzar, Abdelouahd},
  title   = {{AnalyVa}},
  year    = {2026},
  version = {4.2.2},
  url     = {https://analyva.com}
}

RIS

RIS 
TY  - COMP
AU  - Bouzar, Abdelouahd
TI  - AnalyVa
PY  - 2026
ET  - 4.2.2
UR  - https://analyva.com
ER  -

Citing the validation studies

If you rely on the benchmark results (for example, to justify the choice of software in a methods section), cite the relevant study by its Zenodo DOI. All four are open access and immediately citable.

Study 1 — General statistics vs R 4.4.0 + NIST StRD

APA 7 
Bouzar, A. (2026). Validation of AnalyVa's general statistics engine against R 4.4.0 and the NIST StRD certified datasets [Preprint]. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21536912

Example methods paragraph

A short template you can adapt for a manuscript methods section:

All statistical analyses were performed in AnalyVa (version 4.2.2; Bouzar, 2026), an integrated desktop platform combining PLS-SEM, CB-SEM, and general statistics. The software has been benchmarked against R 4.4.0 on the NIST StRD certified datasets (Bouzar, 2026a) and against SmartPLS 4 on the corporate reputation model (Bouzar, 2026b).
