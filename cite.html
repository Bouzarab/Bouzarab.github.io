Citing AnalyVa (the software)

Cite the software any time you use it to produce results in a paper, thesis, dissertation, poster, or report. If you also relied on benchmark evidence from one of the validation studies, cite that study in addition (see below).

✓ Copied to clipboard 


    

      
Bouzar, A., & El Idrissi, K. (2026). AnalyVa (Version 4.2.2) [Computer software]. https://analyva.com

      
Bouzar, Abdelouahd, and Khaoula El Idrissi. AnalyVa. Version 4.2.2, 2026, analyva.com.

      
Bouzar, Abdelouahd, and Khaoula El Idrissi. 2026. AnalyVa (version 4.2.2). https://analyva.com.

Citing the validation studies

  

    If you rely on the benchmark results (for example, to justify the choice
    of software in a methods section), cite the relevant study by its
    Zenodo DOI. All four are open access and immediately citable. Both
    authors — Abdelouahd Bouzar
    (ORCID 0000-0002-6918-7817)
    and Khaoula El Idrissi
    (ORCID 0000-0001-8768-1889) —
    should appear in every citation to the papers.

Example methods paragraph

  

    A short template you can adapt for a manuscript methods section:
  

  

    All statistical analyses were performed in AnalyVa (version 4.2.2;
    Bouzar & El Idrissi, 2026), an integrated desktop platform
    combining PLS-SEM, CB-SEM, and general statistics. The software
    has been benchmarked against R 4.4.0 on the 31 NIST StRD certified
    datasets (Bouzar & El Idrissi, 2026a) and against SmartPLS 4
    on the corporate reputation model (Bouzar & El Idrissi, 2026b).
