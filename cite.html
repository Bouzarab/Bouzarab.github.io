Citation

How to Cite AnalyVa

Copy-paste citations for AnalyVa and the four validation studies. If your reference manager accepts BibTeX or RIS, use those directly.

{% raw %}

Citing AnalyVa (the software)

Cite the software any time you use it to produce results in a paper, thesis, dissertation, poster, or report. If you also relied on benchmark evidence from one of the validation studies, cite that study in addition (see below).

APA (7th edition)

APA 7 
Bouzar, A. (2026). AnalyVa (Version 4.2.2) [Computer software]. https://analyva.com

MLA (9th edition)

MLA 9 
Bouzar, Abdelouahd. AnalyVa. Version 4.2.2, 2026, analyva.com.

Chicago (author-date)

Chicago 
Bouzar, Abdelouahd. 2026. AnalyVa (version 4.2.2). https://analyva.com.

BibTeX

BibTeX 
@software{bouzar_analyva_2026,
  author  = {Bouzar, Abdelouahd},
  title   = {{AnalyVa}},
  year    = {2026},
  version = {4.2.2},
  url     = {https://analyva.com}
}

RIS

RIS 
TY  - COMP
AU  - Bouzar, Abdelouahd
TI  - AnalyVa
PY  - 2026
ET  - 4.2.2
UR  - https://analyva.com

Citing the validation studies

If you rely on the benchmark results (for example, to justify the choice of software in a methods section), cite the relevant study by its Zenodo DOI. All four are open access and immediately citable. Both authors — Abdelouahd Bouzar (ORCID 0000-0002-6918-7817) and Khaoula El Idrissi (ORCID 0000-0001-8768-1889) — should appear in every citation to the papers.

Example methods paragraph

A short template you can adapt for a manuscript methods section:

All statistical analyses were performed in AnalyVa (version 4.2.2; Bouzar, 2026), an integrated desktop platform combining PLS-SEM, CB-SEM, and general statistics. The software has been benchmarked against R 4.4.0 on the 31 NIST StRD certified datasets (Bouzar & El Idrissi, 2026a) and against SmartPLS 4 on the corporate reputation model (Bouzar & El Idrissi, 2026b).
